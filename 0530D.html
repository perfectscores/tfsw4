参考答案

 

"I think communication could become more difficult when working remotely. Sometimes messages and emails are not as clear as face-to-face conversations.

 

In addition, remote workers may have fewer opportunities to build professional relationships, which could affect teamwork and career development over time."

 

10

参考答案

 

"In my opinion, it is fair if the company has reasonable explanations.

 

Different jobs create different opportunities. For example, an accountant may be able to work remotely, but a factory worker cannot. The important thing is that the company communicates openly and treats all employees with respect."

 

11

Great. Some people believe that remote working is the future of work and will become more common. Do you agree or disagree with this idea? Why?

 

参考答案

 

"I partly agree that remote work is the future.

 

I think many companies will adopt hybrid models because they combine the strengths of both approaches. Employees gain flexibility, while businesses still benefit from in-person collaboration. For that reason, I believe hybrid work will become more popular than fully remote work."
