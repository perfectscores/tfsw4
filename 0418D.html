08

参考答案

 

"Currently, I’m trying to follow a simple version of intermittent fasting. This means I usually have my first meal around noon and finish my dinner by 8:00 PM. I find that this schedule helps me improve my digestion and keeps my mind clearer during my morning classes.

 

In terms of what I eat, I prefer light meals like salads, sandwiches, or fruit bowls, especially during the daytime. Since I spend a lot of time sitting and studying, I don't want to feel too heavy or sleepy after lunch. I think having a consistent eating window is a practical way for a busy student like me to manage my health without spending too much time on complex meal prep."

 

09

Thank you. Can you describe some foods that you eat on a regular basis?

 

参考答案

 

"In my daily life, rice and noodles are definitely the foods I eat most often as my main source of carbohydrates. I usually have them with different types of stir-fried dishes, like beef with peppers or tofu with vegetables. I think this is a very traditional and satisfying way to eat.

 

Because I’m often in a hurry, I also eat quite a bit of fresh fruit, like apples or oranges, as a quick snack between meals. Sometimes, if I don't have time to cook, I’ll have a whole-wheat sandwich with some tuna or salad inside. These are my 'go-to' foods because they are easy to find at the university canteen and they provide the balance I need to stay focused."

 

10

Interesting. Tell me about a time when you or someone you know made a significant change to their diet. What was the change and what motivated you or the person you know to make it?

 

参考答案

 

"A close friend of mine made a big change to his diet recently. He used to eat a lot of fast food and red meat, but he decided to switch to a plant-based diet for five days a week.

 

He was motivated by a health check-up where the doctor told him his cholesterol was a bit high. He was quite worried, so he started learning how to cook vegetarian meals like lentil soup and roasted vegetables. It was a significant lifestyle shift for him because he used to dislike greens. Now, he feels much lighter and has actually lost some weight. Seeing his progress also inspired me to be more careful about what I eat. It showed me that even a simple change in food choices can have a massive impact on our well-being."

 

11

Great, some people say they have difficulty maintaining a healthy diet because they do not like the taste of healthier foods. What do you think about that argument? Why?

 

参考答案

 

"I can see the logic in that argument because our taste buds are often addicted to processed foods, which are full of salt, sugar, and fat. When we are used to those strong flavors, natural and healthy foods can seem a bit bland and unexciting at first.

 

But I believe that our tastes can change over time. If you commit to eating cleaner for a few weeks, your tongue becomes more sensitive to the natural sweetness of fruits and the fresh flavors of vegetables. It’s like a training process for your brain. Personally, I think it’s a temporary challenge. Once you break the habit of eating junk food, you’ll realize that healthier options actually make you feel much better and more satisfied in the long run."

【You have volunteered for a research study about personal organization habits. You will have a short online interview with a researcher. The researcher will ask you some questions.】

 

08

Thanks for taking the time to speak with us today. I'd like to ask you some questions about your personal organization habits. First, do you feel more comfortable when things are organized, or does it not matter much to you? Why?

 

参考答案

 

"To be honest, it doesn't matter that much to me if things are perfectly organized all the time. I’m more of a 'functional' person, meaning as long as I can find what I need, a little bit of mess doesn't bother me at all.

 

I feel that spending too much time on organizing every small detail can sometimes be a distraction from actual work. Sometimes, a slightly messy desk even makes me feel more creative and relaxed, as if I have the freedom to think without too many rules. While I do a big clean-up once a week, I’m generally comfortable in a more casual environment. I’d rather focus my energy on finishing my projects than on making sure my pens are in a straight line."

 

09

Fair enough. Now, what is one habit or routine? You think can be helpful for people who want to be more organized.

 

参考答案

 

"I think a very practical habit is the 'Two-Minute Rule.' The idea is simple: if a task takes less than two minutes to complete—like replying to a short email or putting your dishes in the dishwasher—you should do it immediately instead of adding it to a to-do list.

 

This routine is incredibly helpful because it prevents small tasks from piling up and becoming a huge, overwhelming mess later. Many people feel disorganized because they are surrounded by tiny, unfinished jobs. By dealing with things on the spot, you keep your environment and your mind clear. It’s a great way to build momentum and maintain a high level of productivity throughout the day."

 

10

Okay, good. Moving on, do you think organization is something people are born with, or something they can learn? Why?

 

参考答案

 

"I believe it’s a combination of both. Some people are born with a more logical mind, which makes it easier for them to categorize information and keep things in order. You can often see this even in small children.

 

However, I strongly believe that environment and education play a bigger role. If a person grows up in a home where organization is valued, or if they take a job that requires strict deadlines, they will adapt and learn to be organized out of necessity. So, while some might have a head start, the ability to stay organized is ultimately a result of discipline and practice. It’s a lifelong process of learning what works best for your own personality."

 

11

Great. Last one. In your opinion, is organization as important in one's personal life as it is in one's work or school life? Why?

 

参考答案

 

"In my opinion, organization is more critical in one's work or school life because there are usually strict deadlines and other people depending on you. If you are disorganized in a professional setting, it can damage your reputation or lead to failure.

 

On the other hand, I think personal life can be a bit more flexible and relaxed. While it’s good to stay organized at home, it’s also the place where we should feel free to be a little messy sometimes. I treat my home as a space to escape from the pressure of rules and schedules. For me, as long as I can find my essentials, the level of organization in my personal life doesn't need to be as high as it is in my academic or professional life."
