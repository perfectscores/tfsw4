"I think the main reason people use social media is entertainment, especially among young people. For example, many of my friends use TikTok or Instagram to watch short videos, follow trends, and relax after a long day of studying.

 

For me, social media is also a way to stay connected with others. I use WeChat to talk with my classmates and family members, and sometimes we share photos, school updates, or interesting posts with each other.

 

Another reason is getting useful information. For example, I follow some accounts about university life, study tips, and news. So I would say social media is not only for fun, but also for communication and learning."

 

10

Interesting. Can you share a positive experience you or someone you know has had while using social media?

 

参考答案

 

"Sure. One positive experience I had was reconnecting with an old friend through Instagram. We had not talked for a long time because we went to different schools, but one day I saw her post and sent her a message.

 

After that, we started chatting again and sharing updates about our lives. It felt really nice because social media made it easy to keep the friendship alive, even though we were not in the same place.

 

This experience made me realize that social media is not only for entertainment. It can also help people maintain relationships and feel connected."

 

11

There's an ongoing debate about social media's impact on face-to-face communication. Some say it decreases the likelihood of making plans to see friends face-to-face, while others argue it makes face-to-face connection easier. What is your opinion and why?

 

参考答案

 

"I think social media can both help and hurt face-to-face communication, but it depends on how people use it. On the positive side, apps like WeChat or Instagram make it much easier to make plans. People can create group chats, choose a time, and share locations immediately.

 

However, I also understand the opposite view. Some people may feel satisfied with only sending messages or reacting to posts, so they may not feel the need to meet friends in person.

 

Personally, I believe social media is just a tool. If people use it actively to arrange real meetings, it improves face-to-face communication. But if they only use it to replace real interaction, then it can weaken relationships."

参考答案

First, local governments should improve emergency communication systems, such as phone alerts and public announcements. This can help people know what to do before a storm or heat wave arrives.
